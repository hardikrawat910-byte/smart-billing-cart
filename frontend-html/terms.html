Shop

SmartCart is a side project that demonstrates a verified self-checkout. By creating an account or using the site you agree to these terms.

Accounts

  • One account per person. Keep your password private; you are responsible for activity on your account.
  • After 5 wrong passwords an account is locked for 15 minutes.

Scanning and checks

  • Every item you add is checked against its barcode or QR code: unknown or unpublished codes are refused.
  • Your bill's exit pass works once. Staff may check your bag at the exit.

SmartCart Wallet

  • Bills are paid from your wallet. The balance is in Indian rupees (one token = ₹1).
  • To add money, get a top-up token on the Wallet page and pay that amount at the counter by cash or UPI. The money is added only when staff confirm the payment.
  • The balance can only be used in SmartCart and earns no interest. Refunds are made by staff to the wallet.

Fair use

Do not tamper with the cart or the exit-gate camera, and do not edit or copy bills or tokens: they are signed, so altered ones are refused and flagged.

Availability

The site runs on free hosting. It may take up to a minute to wake up and may be unavailable at times. As a side project it is provided as is, without guarantees.

Changes

These terms may change as the project develops; the date at the top shows the latest version.
